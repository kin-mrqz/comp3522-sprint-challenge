# Graph modeling report

Generated by `python -m graph.run_all` from the Hugging Face dataset `NotASI/COMP3522-HKO`. This branch does not merge `proj_Tom`. The statistical numbers cited below are the published test metrics in `analysis/outputs/tables/task3_all_metrics.csv` on that branch.

## Graph

CoWIN stations with hourly rainfall and coordinates form an undirected graph. Edges are mutual 4-nearest neighbours, plus any pair closer than 8 km. Weights are a Gaussian kernel whose bandwidth is the median edge length (5.77 km), fixed from geography before any rainfall model is fit. 2 connected components. Stations dropped for missing coordinates: 6110.

- Nodes: 39 (of 40 hourly stations)
- Edges: 221
- Median / max degree: 9.0 / 20
- Agreement of derived station amber (QC = G and rain >= 30 mm) with published `any_station_amber`: — on 0 hours

Mean correlation of wet station-pairs decreases across the 4 distance bins that contain pairs (Spearman rho of those bin means is -1.000). That is a monotone pattern in a handful of bins, not a large-sample test.

Neighbor rain in the same hour (r = 0.635) is stronger than neighbor rain one hour earlier (r = 0.424). Co-occurring rain, not a one-hour delay between neighbors, is the main spatial signal.

Moran's I (permutation p-values use 200 sampled hours and 99 station shuffles; the mean itself uses every eligible hour):

- wet_season: mean Moran's I 0.087 (permutation p 0.010, n = 13164)
- raining: mean Moran's I 0.140 (permutation p 0.010, n = 3372)

CEDD's 90 daily gauges are a second map and correlogram only. Daily totals cannot reproduce the hourly 30 / 50 / 70 mm rules, so they are not a second predictor.

## Evaluation contract

Decision times split as train before 2024-07-01, validation from 2024-07-01 until 2025-01-01, and test from 2025-01-01. Thresholds for scored models are chosen on validation CSI over alert rates 0.5% to 50%. Persistence keeps the raw 0/1 flag (threshold 0.5). Test metrics are POD, FAR, CSI, ETS, and PR-AUC.

Features at hour t use rainfall and Open-Meteo at or before t. The amber label is the station state at t+lead. Calendar fields use the target hour. QC flags other than G stay missing. They are not filled with zero rain. `proj_Tom` filled the collapsed territory series with zero, so the CSI comparison is not a pure architecture contest. Open-Meteo is a nearest-station covariate, not the label. Logistic regression uses liblinear (L2, tolerance 1e-4, at most 500 iterations). The station-hour design matrix is a few hundred thousand rows, and this solver reaches a usable fit without the saga run that was still moving after 2,000 steps. The penalty and class weighting match the territory logistic models.

Published `proj_Tom` 1-hour territory amber persistence CSI is 0.373. That is the number a graph model has to live next to, not a claim that the two pipelines saw identical rows.

## Task 1 spatial forecast error

The 9-day forecast is one territory-wide max temperature. The node value is that forecast minus the station's observed daily max. Because the forecast is constant across stations, Moran's I of the error on a day equals Moran's I of the observed maxima, so that statistic cannot change with lead. Coordinates in `hko/stations.csv` are marked `coordinates_verified=FALSE`, so this is a dependence check on the station-intercept model. Positive Moran's I would mean linked stations share the same forecast error. The `proj_Tom` mixed model gives each station its own intercept and treats those intercepts as independent.

- lead 1 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.54 C, n = 1095 days
- lead 2 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.63 C, n = 1094 days
- lead 3 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.72 C, n = 1093 days
- lead 4 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.80 C, n = 1092 days
- lead 5 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.86 C, n = 1091 days
- lead 6 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 1.92 C, n = 1090 days
- lead 7 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 2.01 C, n = 1089 days
- lead 8 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 2.10 C, n = 1088 days
- lead 9 day: Moran's I -0.129 (p 1.000), mean |error| 2.20 C, n = 1087 days

The forecast is one number for every station, so after demeaning Moran's I of the error equals Moran's I of the observations. Lead cannot move that statistic: it stays near -0.129 while mean absolute error rises from 1.54 C to 2.20 C. The permutation p-value is one-sided for positive clustering, so p = 1 means these errors are not clustered on the graph. Negative I means linked stations tend to miss on opposite sides of the territory forecast. That is a stable station contrast, which a station intercept absorbs. The temperature analysis stays with that intercept.

## Reading the result

On the 2025 test year, 1-hour territory persistence CSI is 0.373. Inverse-distance neighbors reach 0.385. Logistic regression stays beside persistence (own 0.371, graph 0.361). GraphSAGE is lower at 0.306, and XGBoost is lower still (own 0.127, graph 0.123). At 3 hours, inverse-distance neighbors (0.214) and GraphSAGE (0.204) are above persistence (0.192). Neighbor features raise logistic CSI from 0.108 to 0.141 and XGBoost from 0.051 to 0.102. At 6 hours, persistence (0.120), graph logistic (0.120), and GraphSAGE (0.115) are close, and neighbor features still lift XGBoost from 0.034 to 0.084. On stations that are under 30 mm while a neighbor is at or above 30 mm, persistence CSI is 0.000 because that rule only copies the station's own flag. Neighbor features do not raise CSI above the no-neighbor twin on that slice (logistic 0.102 versus 0.110, XGBoost 0.099 versus 0.110).

A higher CSI for `features=graph` than `features=own`, at the same model family, is the graph effect. The slice above is the case persistence cannot see.

## Caveats

- CoWIN is a community and school network, not the HKO automatic weather station network. Labels use `label_basis=any_cowin_station_qc_G`. Cite CoWIN and Lam et al. (2021) with any public use of these labels.
- The known CoWIN gap 2024-04-17 02:00–07:00 stays missing.
- GraphSAGE positive-class weights are capped at 100. Early stopping uses the average of validation PR-AUC for lead-1 station amber and for the 3-hour warning.
- HKO temperature coordinates are approximate. Do not treat the Task 1 map as a surveyed network.
- Leads are 1, 3, and 6 hours. The proposal also named 12 hours. That lead was not run. At 6 hours, CSI has already fallen close to the territory base rate, so a longer lead is unlikely to change the ranking.
